# Board glitches

The mod runs Jak II's board code, so Jak II's board glitches still work. Nothing is patched out.
If one behaves differently from Jak II, that is a bug in the port.

Sources: the [Jak speedrun wiki](https://wiki.jakspeedruns.com/Jetboard) and community guides.

Buttons below are the mod's defaults (PS2 names): Cross jumps, R1 or R2 does tricks, L2 ducks,
D-pad Up gets on and off. See the controls in the README.

| Glitch | How to do it | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hover | In one jump, press R1 with the stick left or right (spin), then cancel it with R1 and the stick up or down (flip), then again; keep a steady rhythm | Endless height and air time |
| Extended front flip | Do a flip (R1, stick up or down), then cancel it with R1 and stick up | A long jump |
| Free fall | Mash Cross as a hover ends | Steer the board while falling |
| Sideflip | Spin 360 with R1 and stick left or right, end facing forward with the stick held up | A side flip that sets up a hover |
| Stationary hover | Stand still, press Cross, then D-pad Up in the air | A quick hover with no speed |
| Turn hover | Start a hover at an angle with the stick centred | A hover that turns |
| High jump | Hold L2, then press Cross | A higher jump |

## Not in Ratchet & Clank

Skips that rely on Jak II's levels, loading or story (tomb skips, race shortcuts) have no place here.
The ride also ends on water and on hazard ground, so a glitch cannot carry you over those.
